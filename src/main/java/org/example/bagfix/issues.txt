Product Synchronization
The following issues were identified during the product synchronization process:

1 - Synchronization takes too long when processing a large number of products.
2 - Some products remain unsynchronized, even though they already exist in the external system.
3 - When an error occurs, it is difficult to determine which product caused the issue and exactly what happened.
4 - An error with a single product should not halt the synchronization of the remaining products.
